**##Creative Directive**  
The DIM remains locked until the choosen date.Before the unlock date, attemts to access the DIM are met with a playful response that builds curiousity.

Once the timer unlocks,a final verification question pops up. The verification question is deliberately rigged. When the recipient attempts to select the "wrong" answer, the button moves away from the cursor, creating a physical interaction.

Once access is granted, the DIM transitions into a simulated version of the recipient's phone. The lockscreen displays the recipient's photograph as the wallpaper. The recipient is asked to swipe to unclock, and they are presented with a passcode interface and enter a personalized code.Once unlocked, the simulated phone reveals its home screen.

As the recipient enters the passcode, each digit produces a subtle musical tone. The individual tones gradually form a short birthday melody as the passcode is completed. When the final digit is entered, the phone unlocks and the screen briefly transitions into a birthday reveal. Confetti bursts across the screen as a message appears. HAPPY BIRTHDAY [NAME].  The celebration remains on the screen briefly before fading away. The simulated phone then transitions naturally to its home screen. 

The home screen contains a collection of notifications inspired by familiar applications. The notifications are stacked naturally, allowing the recipient to explore them individually. Each notification contains a different piece of the birthday experience.  
-Photos(Google)  
-Music(Spotify)  
-Memories(Snapchat)  
-Notes(Notes)  
-Messages(Whatsapp)  
-Unknown notification  
The recipient is free to explore the cards, open then , return to the home screen and continue discovering what has been prepared.The recipient does not know some of them are deliberately placed within the experience to trigger events.

While the recipient is exploring the notifications, the experience, suddenly changes. An unexpected messages appears from an unkwown number. Before the recipient has time to fully understand the message, an incoming call appears. The phone begins ringing with the recipients favorite song as the  ringtone. The recipient is given the choice to answer or decline. The intended experience is for them  to answer.

When the call is accepted, the interface changes from the simulated phone into a focused call screen. A personal birthday message begins playing. The voice message feels as though someone has genuinely called the recipient to wish them a happy birthday.The message may be recorded by the person who commissioned the DIM or another person choosen by them.

When the birthday message ends, the simulated phone shows a preview of the ended call, just like after a normal call. Then a low battery warning appears on the screen. The recipient dismisses the warning. Another warning appears, then the phone begins shutting down. The screen fades to black. For a brief moment there is nothing,then the final message appears. HAPPY BIRTHDAY [NAME]
