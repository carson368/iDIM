WORLD LEMUR DAY

World Lemur Day is celebrated on every last Friday of the month of October, today. Lemurs are a unique group of primates that are found naturally only in Madagascar. They are considered the most endangered group of mammals in the world.

Did you know some lemurs can sing? They communicate using loud calls that can sound surprisingly musical, as well as scents and other social behaviours.
